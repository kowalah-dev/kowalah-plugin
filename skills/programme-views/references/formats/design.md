# Design

A one-page visual: a programme summary on a page, a poster for a team wall, a process
drawn as a map, an infographic for an internal newsletter. Best when the piece has to be
taken in at a glance and travel as an image or a PDF.

**If your assistant offers a design template** (in Claude, a Design artifact), start from
it; the canvas holds one or more boards that can be exported. **Otherwise**, build a
single self-contained web page sized to print on one page, and date it.

## A one-page summary that works

1. **The headline:** one sentence, the thing the reader should remember.
2. **Three to five figures,** each against its target or baseline where one exists, with
   its definition in small type.
3. **Where the business is constrained:** the top two or three, named.
4. **What's next:** the one or two things happening, and who owns them.
5. **Source and date** of every figure, in a footer.

## A process on a page that works

The steps in order from `kowalah_get_process`, each marked as human, AI-assisted or
AI-run, with the constraint highlighted and any planned change shown beside it. Real step
names, so the team recognises their own work.

## Details that matter

- One idea per board. A second message belongs on a second board.
- Steps and handoffs, never people (rule 4 in the skill).
- If the organisation has a design system or brand guide available, use it.
