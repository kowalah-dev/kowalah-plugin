# Slides

For a meeting or a board pack. Travels as a file, so it has to make sense without anyone
presenting it.

**If your assistant offers a slides template** (in Claude, the Slides template), start
from it so the deck can be downloaded. **Otherwise**, write the deck as a slide-by-slide
outline the person can paste into their own tool, with the figures and their sources on
each slide.

## A deck that works

Short: six to ten slides. One message per slide, written as the slide's title.

1. **The headline or the decision.** What the reader should take away or decide.
2. **Progress against the vision measures:** baseline, target, where it is now, target
   date. From `vision.measures`.
3. **What has changed:** processes redesigned, with a measured outcome where one exists.
4. **Where the business is still constrained:** the top units and processes, with numbers.
5. **Whether the work in flight is aimed there:** hits and misses.
6. **What isn't measured yet,** and what's being done about it.
7. **The ask:** the one or two decisions needed, and who makes them.

Put detail in an appendix rather than on the main slides.

## Details that matter

- Titles that state the finding ("Finance close is the constraint"), not the topic
  ("Finance").
- Each figure's source and date in small type on the slide.
- No slide whose only content is coverage.
