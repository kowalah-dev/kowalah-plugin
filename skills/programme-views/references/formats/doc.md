# Doc

For a written update people read, comment on and edit together: a steering group update,
a write-up of one process, a note to a team.

**If your assistant offers a shared document template** (in Claude, the Docs template),
start from it, so readers can comment and the author can update it in place.
**Otherwise**, write it as a document the person can copy into their own tools.

## A steering update that works

1. **Decisions needed:** at the top, with who decides.
2. **Headline:** two or three sentences on where the programme is.
3. **Where the business is constrained, and what's aimed at it:** the cross-reference
   between the backlog and the work in flight.
4. **What changed since the last update:** processes moved forward on readiness, outcomes
   recorded, questions answered.
5. **Against the vision:** each measure with its baseline, target and current reading.
6. **Not yet known:** what isn't measured or mapped, and the plan to fix it.

If there is a previous update, keep its order where it worked: readers find things where
they expect them.

## A process write-up that works

How the process runs step by step, where the time goes (from `diagnosis`), what is
changing and the quality bar on any AI-run step. Written for the people who do the work.

## Details that matter

- Short paragraphs; the decision before the detail.
- Tables for figures, each with its source and date.
