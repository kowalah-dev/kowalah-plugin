# Motion

A short animated walkthrough: how a process runs today, how it will run after a redesign,
or how the programme has moved over a period. Best for explaining a change to the people
it affects, where a chart would leave them with questions.

**If your assistant offers an animation template** (in Claude, a Motion artifact), start
from it; the walkthrough can be exported as a video. It isn't available on every plan, and
an administrator may have switched it off. **Otherwise**, offer a doc with a step-by-step
storyboard instead.

## A walkthrough that works

Under two minutes. Storyboard it with the person first, scene by scene.

1. **The process as it runs now:** steps in order, from `kowalah_get_process`, with where
   the work waits.
2. **The constraint:** the step that holds the rest up, and why.
3. **What changes:** steps eliminated, merged or handed to AI, from the redesign.
4. **The quality bar:** how the AI-run steps are checked.
5. **What stays the same,** and what the team does with the time, if the organisation has
   said.

## Details that matter

- Steps and handoffs on screen, never people (rule 4 in the skill).
- Real step names from the model, so the team recognises their own work.
- An end card with the date of the data and where to find out more.
