# AI programme lead

The person who runs the AI programme day to day and reports on it, usually to a steering
group or a sponsor. Often an admin in Kowalah.

## What they usually need to show

- **A regular programme update** that someone senior can read without opening Claude: where
  the business is constrained, what's in flight against it, what moved since last time and
  what needs deciding.
- **A live view** they and the core team come back to, so the update isn't rebuilt by hand
  each time.
- **Credit where it's due.** Which teams and processes changed, and who built what others
  now use, if the organisation is comfortable naming builders.

## Questions worth asking

- Who reads the update, how often, and what do they decide with it?
- What did the last update look like? If they have one, use its shape where it works.
- Is there anything the reader has asked about that the last update couldn't answer?

## A good first version

A **doc** for the update (see `../formats/doc.md`), built from the rows view and the work in
flight, with the decision at the top. Offer a **dashboard** behind it for the figures that
change week to week.

## Watch for

- Leading with coverage because it's the number that moves most easily. Lead with
  constraints, and with whether the work in flight is aimed at them.
- Reporting activity (things built, people trained) as if it were the result. Activity is
  worth showing as a leading indicator. The result is processes changed, with a measured
  outcome where one exists.
