# Process owner

The person who owns a business process or a function, such as finance, sales or
operations, and decides what AI changes about it. Often core team in Kowalah, working at
the level of their unit.

## What they usually need to show

- **Where their process loses time**: the steps that take longest or wait longest, and the
  constraint that holds the rest up.
- **What's changing and what's staying human**, so their team can see the plan rather than
  guess at it.
- **That AI is held to a standard** in their area: the quality bar on each AI-run step and
  how it's doing against it.
- **Proof the change worked**, once there is some.

## Questions worth asking

- Is this for their own team, their peers, or the person they report to?
- Which process matters most to them right now? Start with one rather than the whole
  function.

## A good first version

A **dashboard scoped to their unit** (see `../formats/dashboard.md`), using the unit's rows:
its processes with readiness, its backlog and its open questions. For their team, a
**Motion** walkthrough of how one process will run after the change can explain it better
than a chart (see `../formats/motion.md`).

## Watch for

- Anything that reads as a verdict on their team's performance. Keep it to steps and
  handoffs (rule 4 in the skill).
- A view of the whole function when only one process is mapped well. Show the well-mapped
  process properly and mark the rest as not yet mapped.
