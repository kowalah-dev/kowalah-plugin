# Team member

Someone who does the work in a process. Usually a member in Kowalah. Members can read the
whole operating model and vision.

## What they usually need

Mostly not a dashboard. More often something that explains **how the work is done here**:

- **How their process runs, step by step**: who it comes from, what they hand on, and what
  good looks like for their part.
- **What's changing**, for a step they work on, and what's been built to help with it.
- **Something to share** with a colleague or a new joiner.

## Questions worth asking

- Is this for them, for a colleague, or for someone joining the team?
- Which step do they work on?

## A good first version

A **Motion** walkthrough or a short **doc** of one process, from `kowalah_get_process`
(see `../formats/motion.md` and `../formats/doc.md`).

## Watch for

- Anything that looks like monitoring. Describe the process, not the people doing it.
- Their own role being framed as the thing to automate. Describe what changes for the step
  and what the person does instead, if the organisation has said.
