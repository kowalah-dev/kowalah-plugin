# Executive sponsor

The senior person accountable for the AI programme to a board or leadership team. Often
not an IT leader: frequently someone who owns a business result, such as revenue, cost or
operations. May rarely open Claude themselves, so what they read has to stand on its own.

## What they usually need to show

- **Which parts of the business have actually changed**, and what that has been worth,
  where a measured outcome exists.
- **Progress against the vision measures** the organisation set itself: baseline, target,
  where it is now, and by when.
- **Where to invest next**, and why there rather than elsewhere.
- **Risks**: anything the board would expect to hear about before it becomes a problem.

## Questions worth asking

- What does the board or leadership team actually ask about AI?
- If they could see one number, what would it be?
- Is this for a meeting (slides) or for reading ahead (a doc)?

## A good first version

**Slides** (see `../formats/slides.md`): a short deck that opens with the decision or the
headline result, then progress against the vision measures, processes changed, where the
constraints still are, and the ask.

## Watch for

- Usage figures standing in for value. If value hasn't been measured, say so plainly and
  show what is being done to measure it.
- Numbers without a baseline. A result is only a result against where things started.
- Language about time saved that will travel. Board material gets forwarded; see rule 5 in
  the skill.
